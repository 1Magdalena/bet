Help & Guide

Open this when you need it. BET works best with concrete, real situations — not polished case studies.

How to use BET

How to ask BET a good question
Describe what is actually happening and what you are trying to decide. Instead of “How can I increase sales?”, explain the real situation.
How to share a useful Experience
What happened → what you did → what happened afterward. Dates, ranges, gains, losses and timing help when you remember them.
What counts as an Experience?
Successes, failures, things that did nothing, reversals, closures, experiments and old turning points can all be useful.
How matching works
BET uses permitted business and problem context to find qualified relevance. If nothing is relevant enough, 0 matches is a valid result.
Voice or text?
Either. Speak naturally if that is faster. A short concrete answer can be enough; BET structures it without changing what you meant.
Privacy & visibility
What BET may use for permitted matching is separate from what other members can see. Private Notes remain inaccessible until you explicitly send a copy to BET.
Live Research
When no qualified Experience exists, BET may ask a small plausible cohort whether they have actually experienced the missing situation.
My Questions, Network & conversations
Questions stay problem-based. Human connections arise from relevant business problems; BET is not an open people directory.
Editing or deleting Experience/data
You can edit or withdraw your Experience. Account deletion is separate from cancelling a future paid subscription.

Payments on BET

Your subscription covers the BET features included in your plan. Official BET charges are shown through your BET account and official payment flow before you accept them. BET will not ask you to send money directly to another member or representative to unlock features included in your plan. If you receive a different or suspicious payment request, do not pay.
