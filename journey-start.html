Start with 2–3 situations you remember.

Think back through this business. Add a few situations that mattered — a decision, problem, experiment, success, failure, loss, change or turning point. They can be recent or from years ago.

Why this matters. Your past Experience can become useful evidence for another business facing something similar today. It also helps BET understand your business history so it can find better matches when you need them.

Write or speak naturally. If you remember the period, amount, range, increase/decrease or how long something took, include it. Estimates are useful. Never guess.
