Welcome to BET

Real business experience. Not generic advice.

1 · Bring BET a real problem or decision

BET finds what actually happened to relevant businesses and people who faced something similar before.

Instead of: “How can I increase sales?”
Try: “Our online sales have fallen for three months despite increasing Meta spend. I'm deciding whether to cut spend or change the agency.”

2 · Tell BET what actually happened

Useful Experience is concrete: what happened, what you did and what happened afterward. Approximate dates, amounts, ranges, increases, decreases and timing help if you remember them. Never guess a number.

Failures matter too. A decision that failed, did nothing or was reversed can be as useful as a success.

3 · Speak or type

You do not need to write a case study. Speak naturally — even a short concrete answer can be valuable. BET preserves your source and structures it without changing what you mean.

4 · Better context creates better matches

The more accurately BET understands what actually happened in your business, the better it can match your Experience with someone who needs it — and match you with Experience that can help you.

What BET may use for permitted matching is separate from what other members can see.
