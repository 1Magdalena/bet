01

The useful part is usually missing.

Business stories tend to end in success or failure. They rarely show what happened in between — the situation, the decision, what was done and what happened next. That is the part BET records and makes useful.

02

Experience, kept in context.

BET is not a feed of opinions. Each Experience keeps the parts that matter when another business later faces a similar decision.

Context

What kind of business was this, and what was true at the time?

Situation

What changed, became difficult or created the decision?

Decision

What did they actually choose to do?

Outcome

What happened after the decision — immediately and later?

03

Bring a real problem.

Ask BET about a business situation you are dealing with. BET looks for sufficiently relevant experience, shows why it is relevant and keeps weak matches out.

04

No ads. No self-promotion. No pay-to-rank.

Relevant experience comes first. A business belongs on its profile; a sales pitch does not belong in an Experience.

05

The experience is human.

BET uses AI to structure, retrieve, compare and synthesise. It does not replace the source. The underlying experience comes from people who were actually there.
