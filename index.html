Why BET exists

The useful part is usually missing.

Business stories tend to end in success or failure. They rarely show what happened in between — the situation, the decision, what was done and what happened next. That is the part BET records and makes useful.

No ads. No self-promotion. No pay-to-rank.Relevant experience comes first.
The BET record

Experience has a structure.

To compare one business experience with another, BET keeps the important parts intact: context, situation, decision and what followed.

01 / CONTEXTWhere were they?

Company, market, scale and the situation surrounding the decision.

02 / SITUATIONWhat changed?

The actual problem, constraint or opportunity the business faced.

03 / DECISIONWhat did they choose?

The decision made — including the alternatives they rejected.

04 / RESULTWhat happened?

The immediate operational or commercial outcome.

05 / LATERAnd then?

The part business stories usually omit: what the decision looked like months later.

When the record is incomplete

When BET has no good match, it looks for one.

BET can ask a small, relevant group of verified business people whether they have lived the situation. Nothing is shown as a match until the experience is captured and qualifies.
