Ask BET / Missing experience

Live Research

When BET has no qualified recorded match, it can ask a small set of verified members who may actually have the missing experience.

1 · No qualified match

BET does not fill the gap with weak results.

If existing Experience does not cross the relevance threshold, the member is not shown low-quality substitutes.

2 · Targeted qualification

Have you experienced this?

Only plausible experience holders receive the request. A “Yes” means the person may have relevant experience — it does not make them a match yet.

3 · Capture and qualify

Real experience first. Match second.

The member describes what actually happened by voice or text. BET structures it faithfully and applies the same relevance threshold as any other Experience.

4 · Notify only when value exists

A qualified match is now available.

If the new Experience qualifies, the original asker is notified. Otherwise the question remains active for future re-matching.
